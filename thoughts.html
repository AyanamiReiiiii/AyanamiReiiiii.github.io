On pre-college math education

August 2026

We teach students to compute long before we teach them to ask questions. The best math classes I've seen invert this: the problem comes first, the technique arrives when it's needed, and the answer matters least of all. I'd like to write more about what such a class could look like.

Why write exposition?

July 2026

Writing up a topic is the most honest test of whether you understand it. The gaps you can wave past in conversation become impossible to ignore on the page.
