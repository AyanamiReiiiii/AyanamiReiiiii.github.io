Why I Hand-Write My Website

August 2026

This site has no framework, no build step, no package manager warning me about 1,247 dependencies. It's a handful of HTML files and one stylesheet. That's the whole point.

When everything is hand-written, every line is something I understand. If the loading animation on the front page breaks, it's my bug in my twenty lines of JavaScript — not a version conflict buried three dependencies deep.

There's also something pleasantly mathematical about it: the site is small enough to hold in your head all at once, the way a good proof should be.

Will this scale to a hundred posts? Probably not. Will I cross that bridge when I get to it? Absolutely.
