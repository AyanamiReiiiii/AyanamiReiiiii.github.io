Why Write Exposition?

July 2026

Writing up a topic is the most honest test of whether you understand it. In conversation you can wave your hands past the hard step; on the page, the gap sits there in black and white, refusing to close itself.

Every expository piece I've written has taught me something I thought I already knew. Usually it happens around the second draft, when I try to say why a definition is the right one and discover I only ever knew that it was.

And there's a service component: someone is one Google search away from the explanation you owed them. Write it once, write it well, and it works forever.
